## A note for anyone replacing these

Free stock photography of South African stokvels does not exist — a search for
"stokvel" on Unsplash returns zero results. The closest matches (South African
Rands being counted at a table, also by Sincerely Media) sit behind the paid
Unsplash+ licence.

Two traps worth knowing:

- **Real is not the same as relevant.** Several free photos of "a group of people
  smiling" turn out to be colour-run selfies or European apartment portraits.
  Check who is actually in the frame before using it.
- **Verify every licence individually.** Some work by the same photographer is
  free and some is Unsplash+ (`plus.unsplash.com`, "Licensed under the Unsplash+
  License"). The same search page mixes both.

If you have photographs of your own stokvel, use them instead. Nothing on this
site will look more like the people it is for.
